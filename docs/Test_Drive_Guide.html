Test Drive Checklist

SiennaWoodland AWD

Doors & Access

  • Slide both doors from outside — feel power mechanism
  • Slide both doors from inside — test button location
  • Walk through middle gap (where car seats will sit)
  • Install/remove car seat — note ease of access
  • Open rear hatch — visualize double stroller

Driver Experience

  • Tushar drives — highway feel, body roll, steering weight
  • Tanvi drives — seat height, visibility, reach to controls
  • Drive over a bump — note ride smoothness
  • Drive at 60+ mph — note road/wind noise level
  • Tight turn or parking — feel maneuverability

Interior

  • Sit in 2nd row — legroom with car seat ahead of you
  • Sit in 3rd row — legroom, headroom, recline
  • Check infotainment — CarPlay connect, screen size
  • Check cargo area — measure stroller mentally
  • Check all storage — cup holders, door pockets, center console

Practical Checks

  • AWD confirmed on window sticker (Woodland = standard)
  • Confirm it IS a hybrid (all Siennas are — just verify)
  • Check under 40,000 miles
  • Request Carfax / service history
  • CPO (Certified Pre-Owned) warranty status
GHHybrid XLE AWD

Doors & Access

  • Open rear swing doors — note swing arc needed
  • Test car seat install in 2nd row captain chair
  • Try to reach 3rd row with 2nd row car seat installed
  • Open rear hatch — visualize stroller + bags together
  • Note door weight — easy for Tanvi to manage?

Driver Experience

  • Tushar drives — highway feel, body roll, steering weight
  • Tanvi drives — high seating position, visibility, reach
  • Drive over a bump — note ride quality vs Sienna
  • Drive at 60+ mph — compare road noise to Sienna
  • Tight parking — feel the size difference

Interior

  • Sit in 3rd row — test 36.1" legroom at 5'8"
  • Test 3rd row recline — is it meaningfully better?
  • Check infotainment — CarPlay, screen, ease of use
  • Assess cargo with 3rd row up vs Sienna (20.6 cu ft)
  • Feel interior material quality vs Sienna

Practical Checks

  • Confirm XLE AWD trim (not FWD XLE)
  • Confirm HYBRID version (not gas-only GH)
  • Confirm 2024 model year
  • Request Carfax / service history
  • CPO (Certified Pre-Owned) warranty status
